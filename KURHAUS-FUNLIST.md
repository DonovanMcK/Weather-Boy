# Kurhaus — Fun List

Idea capture for the Kurhaus map ("The Aether Baths"). **Capture only — nothing
here is built yet.** Organized by priority tier so this reflects what's worth
doing, not ten equal to-dos. Build items from Tier 1 during the dressing pass;
Tier 2 only on a light session; Tier 3 needs an explicit "is this worth it"
decision before anyone touches it.

---

## TIER 1 — worth building (high payoff, cheap; do during the dressing pass)

- **Spa PA announcer + lobby muzak during rounds.** Calm resort voice over
  elevator music: *"Willkommen to Kurhaus, the thermal baths are a refreshing
  forty degrees, please do not feed the test subjects."* Highest
  value-per-effort on the whole list — gives the map a personality for the cost
  of a few audio clips.
- **"Spa Water" fountain perk** — the dead Courtyard fountain as a one-off
  random-buff drink. Drink → a random low-tier buff; sometimes nothing,
  sometimes great. Tiny scope, players interact with it every match.

---

## TIER 2 — nice-to-have / pure flavor (build only on a light session, never required)

- **Spa-zombie cosmetics:** swim caps, inner tubes, bathrobes, towel turbans,
  arm floaties, one slipper.
- **Bademeister reskin** — the heavy reskinned as a furious lifeguard: whistle,
  swim trunks, torn float ring, deck-chair weapon.
- **Squeaky rubber duck** in the Hot Springs (pick up → squeak; hide a second
  one somewhere stupid).
- **"DO NOT DISTURB" door sign** — one-use found item; zombies path around that
  entrance for ~15s.
- **Massage chair** you can sit in — slow passive health regen, but you can't
  shoot while seated.
- **Achievement toasts** — "NO RUNNING", "TOWEL SERVICE", "CHECKOUT TIME".
- **Robe Mode** — tiny chance you spawn a round in bathrobe + slippers:
  +movement, −armor.

---

## TIER 3 — DEFERRED, likely not worth it (real engineering hiding behind a fun idea — decide explicitly before building, do NOT build casually)

- **Maelstrom Driver "Rime" mode freezes the Cold Plunge into walkable ice.**
  ⚠ This is a **dynamic terrain state that changes collision AND the navmesh
  mid-game** — real work and a real new bug surface for a one-room gimmick.
  Parked until someone decides it's worth the cost.
